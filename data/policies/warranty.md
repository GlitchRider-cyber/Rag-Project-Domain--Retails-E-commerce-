# Aurelia & Co. Craftsmanship Warranty

*Last updated: November 2026*

## Our Promise

Every Aurelia & Co. product is crafted to the highest standards of quality and durability. We stand behind our work with a **1-year Craftsmanship Warranty** that covers defects in materials and workmanship from the date of original purchase.

## What's Covered

This warranty covers manufacturing defects that arise under normal use during the first **12 months** from the date of purchase. Covered defects include, but are not limited to:

- **Seam failures** — stitching that unravels or separates under normal wear
- **Hardware defects** — zippers, buttons, buckles, or clasps that break or malfunction without external cause
- **Material defects** — fabric tearing, leather cracking, or pilling that occurs beyond what is considered normal wear for the material type
- **Color defects** — abnormal or excessive color fading, bleeding, or transfer that occurs despite following the provided care instructions
- **Sole separation** on footwear under normal walking conditions
- **Structural failures** — handles, straps, or frames on bags and accessories that break under normal use

## What's Not Covered

The warranty does **not** cover damage resulting from:

- **Normal wear and tear** — gradual changes in appearance that occur naturally over time (e.g., leather patina development, minor pilling on knitwear, softening of fabric)
- **Misuse, abuse, or accidents** — burns, cuts, punctures, chemical exposure, or damage from external forces
- **Improper care** — failure to follow the care instructions provided on the product label or our website (e.g., machine washing a dry-clean-only garment)
- **Unauthorized alterations or repairs** — modifications made by anyone other than Aurelia & Co. or an Aurelia-authorized repair service
- **Cosmetic preferences** — dissatisfaction with color, fit, or style that does not constitute a defect
- **Water damage** to items not specifically marketed as water-resistant
- **Pet damage**
- **Items purchased from unauthorized resellers** — our warranty is valid only for products purchased directly from aurelia.co, Aurelia & Co. retail stores, or authorized retail partners

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

If you believe your Aurelia & Co. product has a covered defect, follow these steps:

1. **Contact our Customer Care team** at **care@aureliaandco.com** with the subject line "Warranty Claim — [Your Order Number]."
2. **Provide the following information:**
   - Your name and order number (or proof of purchase)
   - The product name and SKU
   - A detailed description of the defect
   - **Clear photographs** of the defect (at least 3 photos: the overall item, a close-up of the defect, and the care label)
3. **Our team will review your claim** within **3-5 business days** and respond with one of the following outcomes:
   - **Approved for repair:** We will provide a prepaid shipping label for you to send the item to our repair center. Repairs are typically completed within **2-4 weeks**.
   - **Approved for replacement:** If the item cannot be repaired, we will send a replacement in the same style, size, and color (subject to availability). If the exact item is no longer available, we will offer a **store credit** for the full purchase price.
   - **Approved for store credit:** In cases where neither repair nor replacement is feasible, we will issue a **store credit** valid for **24 months**.
   - **Claim denied:** If the defect falls outside the scope of this warranty, we will explain the reason and, where possible, recommend a repair service.

## Warranty Repairs

- All warranty repairs are performed by Aurelia & Co.'s in-house artisans or authorized repair specialists.
- Repair shipping (both ways) within the continental U.S. is covered by Aurelia & Co. at no cost.
- International customers are responsible for the cost of shipping to our repair center; return shipping is covered by Aurelia & Co.
- Repairs that fall outside the warranty (e.g., re-heeling boots after the warranty period) may be available for a fee — contact Customer Care for a quote.

## Extended Care

While our warranty covers the first 12 months, many Aurelia & Co. products are designed to last for years with proper care. We recommend:

- Following the **care instructions** on each product's label and product page
- Storing leather goods with **cedar blocks** or in their dust bags
- Having knitwear professionally **de-pilled** as needed
- Re-waxing waxed cotton garments **annually**
- Conditioning leather items every **3-6 months**

## Limitations

- This warranty is **non-transferable** and applies only to the original purchaser.
- Aurelia & Co.'s total liability under this warranty shall not exceed the **original purchase price** of the warranted item.
- This warranty gives you specific legal rights. You may also have other rights that vary by state or country.
