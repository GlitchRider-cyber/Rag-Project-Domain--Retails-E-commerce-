# Aurelia & Co. Privacy Policy

*Effective date: November 1, 2026*
*Last updated: November 2026*

## Introduction

Aurelia & Co. ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (aurelia.co), use our mobile application, visit our retail stores, or interact with us through other channels.

By using our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

When you interact with Aurelia & Co., you may voluntarily provide us with:

- **Account information:** Name, email address, password, phone number, and date of birth when you create an account
- **Purchase information:** Billing address, shipping address, payment method details (processed securely through our payment processor — we do not store full credit card numbers)
- **Communication preferences:** Email opt-ins, SMS consent, and preferred communication channels
- **Customer service interactions:** Information you share when contacting us via email, phone, live chat, or social media
- **Aurelia Rewards information:** Membership details and reward program activity
- **Survey and feedback responses**
- **Gift card** recipient names and email addresses
- **Wishlist** and saved product preferences

### Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website or use our app, we automatically collect:

- **Device information:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, and unique device identifiers
- **Usage data:** Pages visited, products viewed, time spent on pages, click patterns, search queries, and referring URLs
- **Location data:** Approximate location based on IP address (precise location only with your explicit consent)
- **Transaction data:** Purchase history, order amounts, and shopping cart contents

### Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

- **Social media platforms** (when you log in via social accounts or interact with our social media pages)
- **Marketing partners** (demographic and interest data to improve our advertising)
- **Payment processors** (transaction confirmation and fraud prevention data)
- **Shipping carriers** (delivery status and address verification)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- **Process and fulfill orders:** Manage purchases, payments, shipping, and returns
- **Manage your account:** Maintain your Aurelia & Co. account and Aurelia Rewards membership
- **Communicate with you:** Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer service responses
- **Personalize your experience:** Recommend products, remember preferences, and customize content
- **Marketing:** Send promotional emails, SMS messages, and targeted advertisements (only with your consent)
- **Improve our products and services:** Analyze shopping patterns, conduct research, and enhance our website and app
- **Prevent fraud:** Detect and prevent fraudulent transactions and unauthorized account access
- **Legal compliance:** Meet our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

### Types of Cookies We Use

| Cookie Type | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| **Essential** | Required for site functionality (cart, checkout, login) | Session |
| **Performance** | Analytics to understand how you use our site | Up to 2 years |
| **Functional** | Remember your preferences (language, region, size) | Up to 1 year |
| **Marketing** | Deliver relevant ads and track campaign effectiveness | Up to 2 years |

### Managing Cookies

- You can manage your cookie preferences through our **Cookie Consent Banner** displayed on your first visit.
- Most browsers allow you to **block or delete cookies** through their settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.
- To opt out of interest-based advertising, visit:
  - [Digital Advertising Alliance](http://www.aboutads.info/choices)
  - [Network Advertising Initiative](http://www.networkadvertising.org/choices)

### Other Tracking Technologies

- **Pixels and web beacons:** Used in emails and on our website to measure engagement
- **Local storage:** Used to remember your preferences and shopping cart contents
- **Session replay tools:** We may use tools like Hotjar to understand how users interact with our site (these tools anonymize sensitive data)

## How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

### Service Providers

Third parties who perform services on our behalf, including:

- **Payment processing:** Stripe, PayPal
- **Shipping and fulfillment:** USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL
- **Email marketing:** Klaviyo
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics (with IP anonymization enabled)
- **Customer support:** Zendesk
- **Cloud hosting:** Amazon Web Services (AWS)

These providers are contractually obligated to use your information only to perform services for us and to protect your data.

### Other Situations

We may also share information:

- **With your consent** — when you explicitly agree to share data with a third party
- **For legal reasons** — to comply with law, regulation, legal process, or government request
- **To protect rights** — to enforce our terms, protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, and that of our customers and the public
- **Business transfers** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you will be notified of any change in ownership or use of your information)

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3
- **PCI compliance:** Our payment processing is PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant
- **Access controls:** Employee access to personal data is limited to those who require it and is protected by multi-factor authentication
- **Regular audits:** We conduct annual security assessments and penetration testing
- **Data minimization:** We collect only the data we need and retain it only as long as necessary

While we strive to protect your information, no system is 100% secure. If you become aware of any unauthorized access to your account, please contact us immediately.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as:

- Your account is active
- Needed to provide you with our services
- Required by law (e.g., tax and financial records are retained for **7 years**)
- Necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time (see "Your Rights" below).

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability:** Request a machine-readable copy of your data
- **Opt-out:** Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time via the link in any email or by contacting us
- **Restrict processing:** Request that we limit how we use your data
- **Object:** Object to processing of your data for certain purposes, including direct marketing
- **Withdraw consent:** Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

### How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **privacy@aureliaandco.com** or call **1-888-AURELIA**. We will respond to verified requests within **30 days** (or sooner, as required by applicable law).

### California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act, including:

- The right to know what personal information we collect, use, and share
- The right to delete personal information
- The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information
- The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

We do **not** sell personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

### European Economic Area Residents (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, our legal bases for processing your information include consent, contract performance, legal obligation, and legitimate interest. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of **16**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
