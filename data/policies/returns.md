# Aurelia & Co. Return Policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

## Overview

At Aurelia & Co., we want you to love every piece you bring home. If something doesn't work out, we offer a straightforward **30-day return policy** from the date of delivery. Whether it's not the right fit, color, or simply not what you expected, we're here to make the process seamless.

## Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, items must meet **all** of the following criteria:

- Returned within **30 days** of the delivery date (as confirmed by tracking)
- In **original, unworn condition** — no signs of wear, washing, alteration, or damage
- With **all original tags attached** and in their original packaging (dust bags, boxes, etc.)
- Accompanied by the **original receipt or order confirmation email**

### Items That Cannot Be Returned

The following items are **not eligible** for return under any circumstances:

- Items marked as **"Final Sale"** at the time of purchase
- **Gift cards** and e-gift cards
- Items that have been **altered, tailored, or monogrammed**
- **Intimates and swimwear** (for hygiene reasons)
- **Candles** that have been burned or opened
- Items purchased during **warehouse sale events** (unless defective)

## Return Process

Returning an item is simple:

1. **Initiate your return** by logging into your Aurelia & Co. account at [aurelia.co/returns](https://aurelia.co/returns) or contacting our Customer Care team at **care@aureliaandco.com**.
2. **Select the item(s)** you wish to return and provide a reason.
3. **Print your prepaid return label.** A complimentary return shipping label will be emailed to you within 1 business day (domestic U.S. orders only).
4. **Pack the item(s) securely** in the original packaging. Include the packing slip with the return reason indicated.
5. **Drop off the package** at any USPS or UPS location using the prepaid label.

> **Tip:** We recommend using a trackable shipping method and keeping your tracking number until your refund is processed.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, you will be notified via email:

- **Processing time:** Refunds are typically processed within **5-7 business days** of receiving your return at our warehouse.
- **Refund method:** Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used at checkout.
- **Credit card refunds** may take an additional **3-5 business days** to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.
- **Original shipping charges** are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defect or an error on our part.
- **Sale items** purchased at a reduced price will be refunded at the **price paid**, not the original retail price.

## Exchanges

We want to make sure you get exactly what you love:

- **Direct exchanges** are available for a different **size or color** of the same item, subject to availability.
- To request an exchange, follow the same return process and select **"Exchange"** as your return reason.
- If the desired size or color is unavailable, we will process a **full refund** instead.
- Exchange shipments are sent via **Standard Shipping** at no additional cost within the continental U.S.
- Exchange orders are typically shipped within **2-3 business days** after we receive your returned item.

## Final Sale Items

Items marked as **"Final Sale"** are sold at a significant discount and are **not eligible for return, exchange, or store credit**. Final Sale status is clearly indicated on the product page and at checkout. We encourage customers to review sizing guides and product details carefully before purchasing Final Sale items.

Examples of items commonly marked as Final Sale include:
- End-of-season clearance items
- Archive collection pieces
- Items discounted 50% or more from original price

## International Returns

We welcome returns from our international customers, with the following guidelines:

- International returns must be initiated within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- **Return shipping costs** for international orders are the **responsibility of the customer** and are non-refundable.
- We recommend using a **trackable and insured** shipping method, as Aurelia & Co. is not responsible for return packages lost in transit.
- **Customs duties and taxes** paid at the time of import are non-refundable by Aurelia & Co. — please contact your local customs office for duty refund procedures.
- International refunds are processed in **USD** and converted by your bank at the current exchange rate. Any currency conversion differences are not covered by Aurelia & Co.
- International returns should be shipped to:

  742 Lexington Avenue, Suite 200
  New York, NY 10065
  United States
