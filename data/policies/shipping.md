# Aurelia & Co. Shipping Policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

## Domestic Shipping (Continental U.S.)

We offer three shipping options for orders delivered within the continental United States:

| Shipping Method | Delivery Time | Cost |
|----------------|---------------|------|
| **Standard Shipping** | 5–7 business days | **FREE** on orders over **$75** · $7.95 for orders under $75 |
| **Express Shipping** | 2–3 business days | **$12.00** |
| **Overnight Shipping** | 1 business day | **$25.00** |

> **Free shipping note:** The $75 threshold is calculated on the merchandise subtotal *after* any discounts or promo codes have been applied, and *before* tax.

### Alaska, Hawaii & U.S. Territories

- Standard Shipping: 7–12 business days · $12.95
- Express Shipping: 3–5 business days · $22.00
- Overnight Shipping is **not available** for these destinations

## Order Processing Time

- All orders are processed within **1-2 business days** from the time the order is placed.
- Orders placed **after 2:00 PM EST** or on weekends/holidays will begin processing the **next business day**.
- Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.
- During peak shopping periods (Black Friday, holiday season, major promotions), processing times may extend to **2-3 business days**.

## Order Tracking

- Once your order ships, you will receive a **shipping confirmation email** with a tracking number.
- Track your order anytime by logging into your account at [aurelia.co/orders](https://aurelia.co/orders) or by clicking the tracking link in your confirmation email.
- Tracking information may take **12–24 hours** to update after your shipping confirmation email is sent.
- We ship via **USPS**, **UPS**, and **FedEx**, depending on order size and destination.

## International Shipping

Aurelia & Co. ships to over **40 countries** worldwide.

| Region | Delivery Time | Cost |
|--------|---------------|------|
| **Canada** | 7–10 business days | $15.00 |
| **United Kingdom & EU** | 8–14 business days | $20.00 |
| **Australia & New Zealand** | 10–18 business days | $25.00 |
| **Rest of World** | 12–21 business days | $30.00 |

### International Shipping Notes

- **Customs duties, taxes, and import fees** are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the shipping cost or product price.
- Delivery times are **estimates** and may vary due to customs processing in your country.
- International orders are shipped via **DHL International** or **FedEx International Economy**.
- We are **not responsible** for delays caused by customs clearance.
- **P.O. Boxes** are not available for international shipments.

## Holiday & Peak Season Shipping

During the holiday season (November 15 – December 31) and major sale events, please note:

- **Processing times** may be extended by 1-2 additional business days.
- **Carrier delays** are possible due to high shipping volumes and are beyond our control.
- For guaranteed holiday delivery, we recommend the following **order-by dates** (updated annually on our website):
  - Standard Shipping: Order by **December 12**
  - Express Shipping: Order by **December 18**
  - Overnight Shipping: Order by **December 22** (before 12:00 PM EST)

## Shipping Restrictions

- We **cannot ship** to P.O. Boxes for Express or Overnight orders.
- **Signature confirmation** is required for orders over **$300**.
- Certain products (e.g., candles, fragrances) may have **shipping restrictions** to specific international destinations due to customs regulations.
- We currently **do not offer** shipping to the following countries: Russia, North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba.

## Missing, Lost, or Damaged Packages

- If your tracking shows **"Delivered"** but you haven't received your package, please wait **48 hours** and check with neighbors and your building's mail room. If still missing, contact us.
- For packages **lost in transit** (no tracking update for 7+ business days), contact our Customer Care team and we will file a claim with the carrier and arrange a **replacement or refund**.
- If your order arrives **damaged**, please photograph the packaging and item(s) and contact us within **48 hours** of delivery. We will arrange a **free replacement** or full refund.

## Gift Shipping

- Select **"This is a gift"** at checkout to include a complimentary gift message and have the packing slip price removed.
- Gift orders are wrapped in our signature Aurelia & Co. tissue paper with a satin ribbon.
- Gift receipts are included for easy exchanges by the recipient.
