# Aurelia & Co. — Frequently Asked Questions

## Q: How do I place an order?
Browse our collections at aurelia.co, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or create an Aurelia & Co. account for a faster experience on future orders. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

## Q: Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?
We begin processing orders quickly, so changes are only possible within **1 hour** of placing your order. Contact our Customer Care team immediately at care@aureliaandco.com or call 1-888-AURELIA. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be modified or cancelled — but you can return it under our 30-day return policy.

## Q: What shipping options do you offer?
We offer three domestic shipping options: **Standard Shipping** (5–7 business days, free on orders over $75), **Express Shipping** (2–3 business days, $12), and **Overnight Shipping** (1 business day, $25). We also ship internationally to over 40 countries. See our full Shipping Policy for details.

## Q: How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also track your order by logging into your account at aurelia.co/orders. Tracking information may take 12–24 hours to update after the shipping confirmation email is sent.

## Q: What is your return policy?
We offer a **30-day return policy** from the date of delivery. Items must be unworn, with all original tags attached, and in their original packaging. Returns are free for domestic orders — we provide a prepaid return shipping label. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving your return. Final Sale items are not eligible for return.

## Q: Do you offer exchanges?
Yes! You can exchange an item for a different size or color of the same product, subject to availability. Initiate an exchange through your account at aurelia.co/returns or contact Customer Care. Exchange shipments are sent at no additional cost within the continental U.S.

## Q: How do I find my size?
Each product page includes a detailed **Size Guide** with measurements in both inches and centimeters. We recommend measuring yourself and comparing to our charts for the best fit. If you're between sizes, our product descriptions include fit guidance — for example, our Highlands Cable-Knit Sweater has a relaxed fit, so you may want to size down if you prefer a more fitted look.

## Q: What if an item is out of stock?
If an item is currently out of stock (like our popular Seraphina Velvet Midi Dress or Luna Silk Robe), you can click **"Notify Me"** on the product page to receive an email alert when it's restocked. Aurelia Rewards members get early access to restocks before the general public.

## Q: Do you offer gift cards?
Yes! We offer **digital gift cards** in denominations of $50, $100, $200, and $500. Gift cards are delivered via email and never expire. They can be used for any purchase on aurelia.co, including sale items. Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash.

## Q: What is Aurelia Rewards?
**Aurelia Rewards** is our loyalty program, and it's free to join! Members earn **1 point per $1 spent** on every purchase. Here's how the tiers work:

- **Member** (0–499 points): Earn points, birthday reward, early access to sales
- **Gold** (500–999 points): All Member benefits + free express shipping, exclusive seasonal gifts
- **Platinum** (1,000+ points): All Gold benefits + complimentary alterations, private shopping events, early access to new collections and restocks

Points can be redeemed as store credit: **100 points = $10 off**. Points expire after **12 months** of account inactivity.

## Q: How do I join Aurelia Rewards?
Simply create an account at aurelia.co/rewards. You can also join at checkout — any purchase made before joining can be retroactively credited to your account within 30 days by contacting Customer Care with your order number.

## Q: Do you have a warranty on your products?
Yes. All Aurelia & Co. products come with a **1-year Craftsmanship Warranty** that covers defects in materials and workmanship. This includes seam failures, hardware malfunctions, abnormal material defects, and sole separation on footwear. Normal wear and tear, misuse, and unauthorized alterations are not covered. See our full Warranty Policy for details on how to make a claim.

## Q: How should I care for my cashmere items?
Our cashmere pieces — like the Alpine Cashmere Scarf, Como Cashmere V-Neck Sweater, and Belmont Cashmere Cardigan — are made from Grade-A Mongolian cashmere and deserve gentle care. **Hand wash** in cold water with a cashmere-specific shampoo, gently press out water (never wring), and **lay flat to dry**. Store folded with cedar blocks to deter moths. A cashmere comb can remove any pilling that occurs naturally.

## Q: How do I care for leather products?
For items like the Portofino Leather Tote, Regent Leather Belt, and Kensington Chelsea Boots, **condition with leather balm or cream every 3–6 months** to keep the leather supple. Wipe clean with a damp cloth and avoid prolonged sun exposure. Store bags stuffed with tissue paper and use cedar shoe trees in boots. Full-grain leather develops a beautiful patina over time — this is a sign of quality, not a defect.

## Q: How should I care for silk garments?
Our silk pieces — including the Midnight Silk Wrap Dress, Botanica Silk Camisole, and Luna Silk Robe — require gentle handling. Most silk items should be **dry cleaned** or **hand washed in cold water** with a silk-specific detergent. Never wring silk; instead, press gently in a clean towel. Hang to dry away from direct sunlight, and store on padded hangers. If ironing is needed, use a low heat setting on the reverse side.

## Q: What materials do you use in your candles?
Our candles — the Côte d'Azur Soy Candle and Provençal Lavender Candle — are made from **100% natural soy wax** with **lead-free cotton wicks** and scented with **pure essential oils or phthalate-free fragrances**. They contain no synthetic dyes, paraffin, or harmful chemicals. Each candle is hand-poured in small batches in the USA.

## Q: What is Aurelia & Co.'s approach to sustainability?
Sustainability is central to our mission. Key initiatives include:

- **Materials:** We prioritize natural, organic, and recycled materials — like the recycled polyester in our Isabelle Pleated Midi Skirt and organic cotton in our Mayfair Chinos and sheet sets.
- **Sourcing:** Our merino wool comes from RWS-certified (Responsible Wool Standard) farms, and our cotton is GOTS or Oeko-Tex certified.
- **Packaging:** All shipping boxes are made from 100% recycled cardboard. Tissue paper is FSC-certified, and we've eliminated single-use plastic from our packaging.
- **Production:** We partner with factories that meet strict environmental and labor standards, and we audit them annually.
- **Longevity:** We design products to last — our Craftsmanship Warranty and care guides help extend the life of every piece.

## Q: Are your products ethically made?
Yes. We are committed to ethical manufacturing practices across our entire supply chain. All of our production partners are required to meet our **Supplier Code of Conduct**, which covers fair wages, safe working conditions, no child labor, and environmental responsibility. We conduct annual third-party audits of all factories and publish the results in our annual Impact Report, available at aurelia.co/impact.
